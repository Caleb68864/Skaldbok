**Law 9:** ALL weapons; armour: ALL armour. Chance of a **Check** on first approach or each day on the streets: **83%** (2D ≤ 9); acting suspiciously **92%**; breaking and entering → Investigate **97%**. A laser weapon found here is **DM+7** on sentencing (LL − 2).

**Gov 9 — Impersonal Bureaucracy.** Agencies insulated from the governed citizens. Typical contraband: **Technology, Weapons, Drugs, Travellers, Psionics**. **Travellers themselves are restricted** — expect to be confined to the port or watched.

**Inside the port fence:** Imperial law — roughly **LL1**, and **all psionics forbidden**. Locally illegal kit is fine as long as it never leaves the starport.
