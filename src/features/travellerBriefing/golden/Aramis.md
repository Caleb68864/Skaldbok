**Law 6:** All firearms except shotguns & stunners; carrying weapons discouraged. Chance of a **Check** on first approach or each day on the streets: **42%** (2D ≤ 6); acting suspiciously **58%**; breaking and entering → Investigate **72%**. A laser weapon found here is **DM+4** on sentencing (LL − 2).

**Gov 5 — Feudal Technocracy.** Rule by those who perform mutually beneficial technical activities. Typical contraband: **Technology, Weapons, Computers**.

**Inside the port fence:** Imperial law — roughly **LL1**, and **all psionics forbidden**. Locally illegal kit is fine as long as it never leaves the starport.
